## Event Storming — the discovery lens

Explore a domain as a timeline of **domain events**, and the commands, actors,
aggregates, policies and hotspots around them — the collaborative workshop
notation for discovering how a business really works.

Files use the `.storm` extension and open in the Event Storming Modeler, with a
lossless DSL round-trip and a deterministic JSON model.

**Open the Event Storming tutorial** (the button on the left) for a guided
"create your first board" walkthrough from the modeler itself.
