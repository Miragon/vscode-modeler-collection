## Context Maps — the architecture lens

Cut the domain into **bounded contexts** — typed by subdomain (core, supporting,
generic) — and connect them with the context-mapping relationship patterns of
strategic Domain-Driven Design.

Files use the `.cm` (or `.cm.json`) extension and open in the Context Maps
Modeler, interoperable with Context Mapper CML.

**Open the Context Maps tutorial** (the button on the left) for a guided
"create your first diagram" walkthrough from the modeler itself.
